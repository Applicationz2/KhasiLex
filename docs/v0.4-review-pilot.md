# KhasiLex v0.4 — 100-Entry Review Pilot

The first v0.4 corpus milestone is **100 verified Khasi lexical entries**. The purpose is to validate editorial quality and workflow before scaling to thousands of words.

## Why only 100 first

A small, controlled pilot lets KhasiLex measure:

- reviewer consistency;
- headword/variant policy;
- quality of Khasi definitions;
- sense splitting;
- provenance/licensing discipline;
- grammatical classification quality;
- treatment of reduplication and multiword constructions;
- effort per verified entry.

The pilot should not simply take the first 100 tokens from a frequency list.

## Candidate pool

Build a pool larger than 100 using several evidence paths:

1. high-frequency candidates from approved Khasi corpora;
2. Khasi lemmas from openly licensed lexicographic resources;
3. existing KhasiLex seed entries;
4. human-proposed everyday Khasi vocabulary;
5. a small number of reduplicative/multiword constructions.

All candidates begin as `pending`.

## Suggested pilot balance

The final 100 verified entries should aim for useful grammatical and semantic coverage rather than an artificial quota. A practical target mix is:

- 20–30 common nouns;
- 15–20 verbs;
- 10–15 pronouns/determiners/function words;
- 10–15 adjectives/adverbs;
- 5–10 conjunctions/particles/prepositions or other grammatical words;
- 5–10 idioms, compounds, multiword expressions or reduplicative constructions;
- remaining slots selected by corpus frequency and everyday usefulness.

If Khasi grammatical analysis requires different categories, the editorial evidence takes precedence over this planning mix.

## Entry review checklist

Before an entry reaches `verified`, confirm:

- canonical spelling and Unicode NFC;
- stable entry ID;
- entry type;
- part of speech / grammatical category;
- Khasi definition written or approved by a Khasi reviewer;
- distinct senses separated where necessary;
- source/provenance;
- licence/permission status;
- reviewer identity;
- review date;
- translation status kept separate from Khasi lexical verification;
- natural example sentence where practical;
- variants/dialect/register noted rather than silently normalized away.

For reduplication also confirm base form, reduplication pattern/type and grammatical function.

## Review sequence

1. **Candidate extraction** — generate a broad pending list.
2. **Deduplication** — compare against the master lexicon and accepted variants.
3. **Lexical review** — spelling, lemma, sense and Khasi definition.
4. **Grammar review** — part of speech, grammatical function and morphology.
5. **Provenance review** — source and reuse permission.
6. **Translation review** — English or other-language equivalents, where included.
7. **Verification** — mark `verified` only after the v0.4 validator passes.
8. **Audit** — run `python scripts/corpus_audit.py` and inspect the report.

## Acceptance criteria

The review pilot is complete when:

- at least 100 entries are `verified`;
- all 100 pass the automated v0.4 verification gates;
- no illustrative seed/licensing placeholder is treated as production evidence;
- every verified entry has Khasi definition, grammar classification, provenance, reviewer and date;
- disputed entries are left `reviewed` or `pending` instead of being forced through;
- the review log records the status changes.

After this milestone, KhasiLex can scale the same workflow toward 1,000 and then 5,000 verified entries.
