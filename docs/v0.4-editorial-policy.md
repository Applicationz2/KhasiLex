# KhasiLex v0.4 Editorial Policy

KhasiLex v0.4 begins the transition from a technical dictionary platform to an authoritative Khasi lexical corpus.

## 1. Canonical headwords

- Store the standard Khasi headword in Unicode UTF-8 and NFC.
- Preserve meaningful diacritics, including `ï` and `Ï`.
- Do not silently modernize, anglicize, de-accent, or respell a source form.
- Put accepted alternate spellings in `variants` rather than duplicating the same lexical sense as unrelated entries.
- Dialect or regional forms should be identified explicitly in `dialect` and linked to the relevant concept/sense.

## 2. Sense separation

A headword with multiple meanings must have separately reviewed lexical senses in the multilingual sense/concept layer. Do not collapse unrelated meanings into one English gloss.

## 3. Reduplication and multiword constructions

Forms such as `biang biang`, `wut wut`, `kloi kloi`, and other repeated or multiword Khasi constructions must be treated as possible grammatical or lexical units, not automatically as duplicate-word errors.

A verified reduplicative entry requires:

- base form;
- reduplication type;
- reduplication pattern;
- grammatical function;
- Khasi definition;
- source/provenance;
- human reviewer and review date.

Partial reduplication and productive morphological rules must be added only when supported by reviewed linguistic evidence.

## 4. Verification states

`pending` — imported, suggested, AI-generated, or otherwise not yet reviewed.

`reviewed` — examined by a competent reviewer but not yet approved as production-authoritative.

`verified` — approved for authoritative dictionary use and passing the v0.4 quality gate.

No automated process may promote an entry to `verified` solely because an AI model or translation service produced it.

## 5. Minimum requirements for verified entries

Every verified entry must include:

- stable entry ID;
- canonical headword;
- entry type;
- part of speech or appropriate grammatical classification;
- Khasi definition;
- source;
- licence/permission status;
- reviewer;
- review date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.

Where an English translation is marked verified, a reviewed English definition/gloss is also required.

## 6. Provenance and copyright

Do not bulk-copy copyrighted Khasi dictionaries or other lexical resources unless their licence explicitly permits the intended reuse and redistribution. Record source and licence information for all production data.

## 7. AI assistance

AI may help propose:

- candidate headwords;
- sense splits;
- draft definitions;
- example sentences;
- grammatical analyses;
- multilingual equivalents.

All such content remains `pending` until human review. KhasiLex uses AI as an editorial assistant, not as the lexical authority.

## 8. Public releases

Public dictionary products should default to `verified` entries. Pending and reviewed data may be exposed only in clearly identified development/editorial interfaces.
