# Khasi ancestral-language research and Batch 003 verification

Status: research protocol only; no lexical promotions or content redistribution authorized.

## Distinct collections
1. **Ki Ktien Tymmen**: traditional proverbs, idioms and sayings. Preserve original wording, spelling variants, literal meaning, figurative interpretation, regional attribution, and exact source.
2. **Ki Jingsneng Tymmen**: traditional moral and social teachings. Record narrator/author, date, context, attribution, and consent where applicable.
3. **Ki Riti Dustur ki Longshuwa**: terminology for ancestral customs, with careful separation of historical description from current practice.
4. **Ki Jingthoh Barim**: historical textual attestations and variant spellings.

The phrase **Jingsnem ki Longshuwa** is not assumed to be a verified publication title; retain as a discovery query only until bibliographic evidence is found.

- Modern Khasi government curricula, newspapers and published usage: exact quotation/URL/date required for a modern-sense attestation.

## Entry verification rules
Each candidate must record: candidate ID, exact headword and variants, source edition, printed page or stable entry URL, inspected excerpt, grammatical category, sense match/ambiguity, contemporary contextual usage, copyright/licence determination, and reviewer decision.

A catalog listing or search-result snippet alone is **not** an inspected entry. A reprint is not a second independent lexical authority. A modern mention of a phrase without its meaning counts as **form-only** attestation. Historical words may remain historically attested even if contemporary usage cannot be demonstrated.
